Getting started

Brixora organizes your journal around properties, units and ledger entries.

  1. Open Properties and add a property with its value, loan and other details.
  2. Add units, occupancy status, rent and lease information.
  3. Use Ledger to record rent collected and costs paid.
  4. Open Overview and Analysis to see calculations based on your entries.

To remove the built-in sample records, open Workspace → Data management → Clear all records. This permanently deletes the current workspace and attached photos.

Exporting your records

You can prepare spreadsheet-compatible CSV files without creating an account.

  1. Open Workspace and find Export.
  2. Select Properties, Units, or Rent and costs.
  3. Tap Prepare file.
  4. Use the Share button to choose a destination.

Exports contain real figures even when Privacy mask is on. CSV exports do not include attached photographs. Review files before sharing them.

Backup and restore

Brixora stores its workspace locally and does not include a separate cloud-backup or cross-device sync service.

  • Export all three CSV types regularly and save them somewhere you trust.
  • Keep separate copies of original leases, receipts and other attached photographs; they are not included in CSV exports.
  • Your device may include app data in its own backups, depending on your system settings.
  • Back up before clearing records, removing the app or replacing your device.

CSV files are intended for export and record keeping. Brixora does not currently offer a CSV import or one-tap restore feature.

Camera and photo permissions

Permissions are optional and are used only when you attach evidence to your own records.

  • Allow camera access to photograph a lease, invoice, receipt or inspection report.
  • Allow photo library access to attach an existing image.
  • If access was denied, open your device’s Settings, find Brixora, and update the available permissions.

You can continue using the journal without these permissions. Attached images are stored locally in the app and are not uploaded to us.

Privacy and security

No sign-in is required. Brixora has no ads, analytics, tracking or app network requests.

  • Turn on Workspace → Privacy mask to hide headline amounts on screen.
  • Use a device passcode and keep your iPhone software up to date.
  • Be careful with exports containing addresses, tenant names and financial figures.
  • Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy mask changes what is displayed; it does not encrypt exports or remove the underlying figures.

Deleting app data

To permanently clear your local journal:

  1. Export anything you want to keep and save original photos separately.
  2. Open Workspace → Data management.
  3. Tap Clear all records and confirm.

This removes the workspace file and every attached photo from the app. It cannot be undone. Previously exported files and device backups are not deleted.

Understanding calculations

Occupancy, NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, debt service coverage and other figures are arithmetic based on your records.

  • Check property values, loans, dates, rent and expense entries if a result looks unexpected.
  • Scenario results depend entirely on the assumptions you provide.
  • Projections are not predictions or guarantees of future performance.

Brixora is a journal, not an adviser, broker, property manager or tax tool. It does not provide financial, legal or investment recommendations.

Troubleshooting

If something is not working as expected:

  1. Close and reopen Brixora, then try the action again.
  2. Restart your iPhone and check for available app or system updates.
  3. For export problems, check that the device has sufficient free storage.
  4. For photo problems, review camera and photo permissions in Settings.

Do not clear your workspace or uninstall the app while troubleshooting unless you have secured the records and photos you want to keep.
