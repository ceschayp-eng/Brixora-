1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Brixora iPhone app and the Brixora informational website. “Brixora,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to the provider of the Brixora app.

The app is a record-keeping and analysis journal. It does not require an account and does not send your property journal to us or to an app-operated server.

2. Information stored locally

Brixora stores the information you choose to enter in the app’s local storage on your device. This may include:

  • Property records: names, addresses, property classes, purchase prices, current values, loan details, rent and other financial figures.
  • Unit and lease records: unit details, occupancy status, tenant names, rent and lease end dates.
  • Ledger entries: rent payments, expenses, dates, payment methods, vendors and notes.
  • Attachments and activity: photographs you attach, portfolio snapshots and records of activity within the app.
  • Settings: currency, privacy mask, occupancy goal and display preferences.

Calculations, charts and projections are performed on your device. We do not receive, access or maintain a copy of this journal data through the app.

3. Camera and photo library access

Camera and photo library permissions are optional. Brixora requests them only when you choose to take or select a photograph for a lease, invoice, receipt, inspection report or other property record.

Selected images are saved in the app’s local storage. Brixora does not upload them to us. You can deny permission and still use the app’s record-keeping and analysis features. You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Exports and sharing

You can prepare CSV files containing property, unit, rent and expense information. A prepared file remains on your device until you choose to share it using the system sharing options.

When you share or save an export, the destination app, cloud service or recipient handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Brixora does not control exported copies. Exports do not include attached photographs. Review exported files carefully because they may contain tenant names, addresses and financial information.

5. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your name, device model, operating system, app version and description of the issue. Your email provider also processes the message according to its own privacy policy.

We use this information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, improve support, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Please do not send tenant records, leases, financial documents or other sensitive journal data unless we specifically need them to resolve your request.

6. This website

This website is static and does not include our own analytics, advertising trackers, account system or cookies. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details and requested pages in server logs to deliver and protect the site.

That processing is governed by GitHub’s policies. You can review the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to external sites are provided for convenience, and their privacy practices are their own.

7. Disclosure of information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. Because app journal data is stored locally and is not transmitted to us, we cannot disclose what we do not possess.

Limited information may be processed by service providers that deliver ordinary infrastructure, such as email and website hosting. We may also disclose support-related information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

8. Retention and deletion

Your app journal

Your journal and attached photos remain in the app until you delete them, clear the workspace or remove the app. In Brixora, go to Workspace → Data management → Clear all records to delete the workspace and attached photos from the device. This action cannot be undone.

Deleting app data does not delete files you already exported or copies stored in device backups. Those copies must be managed separately. Removing the app may delete its local data, so keep your own backup before doing so.

Support emails

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, maintain appropriate business and security records, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. You may ask us to delete a support conversation by emailing us.

9. Security

Local storage reduces exposure, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device with a strong passcode, keep its software updated, and be careful when exporting records or attaching documents that contain tenant or financial information.

Brixora does not provide a separate cloud-backup service. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in a device backup. You control those settings and any destinations you choose for exported files.

10. Children’s privacy

Brixora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app has no account system and journal data stays on the device. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support email, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can directly manage app journal data on your device. Because we do not receive that data, we cannot access, correct, export or delete it for you remotely.

For personal information contained in our support correspondence, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your request, and some rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

12. International processing

If you email us or visit this website, your communication or technical connection data may be processed in countries other than your own by us and our email or hosting providers. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. We handle such information as described in this policy and as required by applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, website or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel where required.
